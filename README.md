# Customer Engagement & Product Utilization Analytics for Retention Strategy

## Project Overview

This project analyzes banking customer engagement, product utilization, financial value, and relationship strength to identify factors associated with customer churn.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer engagement and churn
- Measure product utilization and retention
- Identify high-value disengaged customers
- Analyze relationship strength and churn
- Develop customer risk segments
- Provide customer retention recommendations

## Dataset

The project uses 10,000 banking customer records.

Key variables include:

- CustomerId
- CreditScore
- Geography
- Gender
- Age
- Tenure
- Balance
- NumOfProducts
- HasCrCard
- IsActiveMember
- EstimatedSalary
- Exited

## Key Findings

- Total customers: 10,000
- Churned customers: 2,037
- Overall churn rate: 20.37%
- Active customer churn: 14.27%
- Inactive customer churn: 26.85%
- Strong relationship churn: 12.95%
- Moderate relationship churn: 17.92%
- Weak relationship churn: 32.33%
- High-balance disengagement rate: 49.88%
- High-value churned customers: 592
- Critical churn-risk customers: 380

## Customer Risk Segments

The analysis identified:

- Low Risk
- Disengagement Risk
- Churned Customer
- Critical Churn Risk
